01 · The problemWhy simulate?

Backtests lie.
Nobody can grade the lie detectors.

A backtest can look brilliant because you tried 120 variants and kept the best, because your code peeked at the future, because you forgot trading costs, or because your data only contains the companies that survived.

Statisticians have built many tests to catch this: the Deflated Sharpe Ratio, the Probability of Backtest Overfitting, White's Reality Check, Hansen's SPA, holdouts and placebo re-runs. But in real markets nobody knows which strategies truly work, so nobody can score the tests. You can't measure a detector without an answer key.

So this project builds the answer key. Every market is simulated, every flaw is planted on purpose, and an oracle knows the true edge of every claim.

02 · The setupFive moving parts

Plant the crime.
Then send in the detectives.

One experiment, run once with its rules fixed in advance. The config and code were hashed before the final run; the results are locked.

1

Markets

Four simulated markets with fat tails and volatility clustering (GARCH, Student-t). The truth of each is known: no edge, a real trend, a reversal that costs destroy, or crashing stocks that get delisted.

2

Researchers

Ten complete research processes. Two are honest; eight carry one classic flaw each. Each turns a dataset into a claim.

3

Claims

Only backtests with t ≥ 2 count as claims, because flawed research is only ever shown when it looks good. 300 claims per researcher.

4

Audits

Fourteen audits in three access tiers judge every claim: credible or rejected.

5

Oracle

The ground truth: the same rule, implemented honestly, run on ~2,300 years of fresh data from the same market. A true Sharpe ≤ 0.05 means fake; ≥ 0.20 means real.

Delay, Cost×2 and the point-in-time universe look weak here only because each catches exactly one flaw, almost perfectly, and almost never raises a false alarm. That is Exhibit C.

The Deflated Sharpe with an assumed 100 trials and the forward tests both catch over 85% of fakes. They also reject 65–83% of real edges. A smoke alarm that goes off every time you cook doesn't tell you much.

The Probabilistic Sharpe Ratio sits at zero on both axes. Every claim already passed a significance test, so asking the same question again with a fat-tail correction changes nothing.

Exhibit C · AntidotesImplementation flaws

One flaw, one antidote.
Never the same one twice.

The statistical audits in tiers 1 and 2 caught 0% of these four flaws: each researcher ran a single, honest-looking test, so there is no multiple testing to correct. Each flaw falls instantly to one cheap re-run attack, and every other attack misses it.

Exhibit E · SeparationThreshold-free AUC

PBO is a
coin flip.

Forget pass/fail thresholds. Using each audit's continuous score, how well does it separate a researcher's fake claims from all real ones? 1.0 is perfect separation; 0.5 is a coin toss. The Probability of Backtest Overfitting lands at 0.50.

Median AUC per audit, with the range across flaws
Each flaw's fake claims vs all 600 real claims
Data table

PBO asks whether the in-sample winner keeps winning across 12,870 ways of splitting history in half. A strategy that was lucky across its whole history looks consistent, so PBO passes it.

PBO grades the selection procedure, not whether this particular winner is real. Useful for research hygiene; useless as a verdict on one claim.

The Deflated Sharpe with the true trial count had the opposite problem. When the strategies tried really do differ, it counts the real differences as noise and rejected 100% of genuine mined edges.

05 · VerdictWhat the matrix says

Nine findings.

  1. Returns alone tell you almost nothing.The PSR caught 0%. The Deflated Sharpe with assumed trials caught most fakes only by rejecting 91% of honest real edges, and it caught 0% of look-ahead, whose fake Sharpe of 3.5 clears any bar.
  2. Disclosing every trial fixes selection bias, and only that.On the 120-rule miner: Bonferroni 96%, Reality Check 90%, SPA 89%. Every tier-2 audit scored 0% on look-ahead, leakage, costs and survivorship.
  3. PBO cannot judge a single claim.It caught 25% of mined fakes and 8% of asset-picked fakes, with an AUC of 0.50. It measures how consistent a selection process is, not whether this winner is real.
  4. The Deflated Sharpe punishes genuine variety.With the true trial count it rejected 100% of real mined edges, because real differences between strategies look like noise to it.
  5. Each implementation flaw has its own antidote.Look-ahead: delay 96%. Leakage: placebo 95%. Costs: cost×2 100%. Survivorship: point-in-time universe 96%. No single attack catches all four.
  6. The placebo is the best all-rounder, but it is blind to data and economics.It caught 85–98% of selection and leakage flaws but only 4% of survivorship and 8% of ignored costs, and it wrongly rejected 22% of real mined edges.
  7. Window picking is the hardest selection flaw.Choosing the best of 11 start dates turned zero edge into a claimed Sharpe of 1.05. The best usable audit caught 36%.
  8. Honest luck is invisible without new data.No usable audit catches a lucky honest test. Forward tests catch it, but they also reject 79% of real edges after one year.
  9. Selection inflates even real edges.Real mined edges were claimed at 1.22 against a true 0.69. The t ≥ 2 filter alone took honest real edges from 0.72 to 1.02.

Practical rule: ask for every trial, and also re-run the pipeline with a one-day delay, double costs, delisted names restored, and placebo data. Statistics on the returns alone will not save you.

06 · MethodHow to check this work

Pre-registered.
Hashed. Locked.

Every threshold was fixed before any run; dev runs used a different seed; the final run executed once and wrote a lock file. Every decision, including the ones made after seeing results, is logged with who made it and why.

Integrity

  • Final config hash b39faa28…
  • Code hash at run time a8897be1…
  • Seed 314159265, distinct from dev
  • 84 automated tests, including causality checks proving the honest engine never sees the future and the planted bugs really do
  • Size checks: RC, SPA and the placebo reject ≤ 8% when there is truly no edge

Caveats

  • The ground truth is exact for these simulated markets, which have no regimes, crowding or structural breaks.
  • Conventional thresholds (α = 5%, PBO > 0.5, DSR < 0.95). The AUC exhibit is threshold-free.
  • One family of simple rules, with 300 claims per researcher (95% intervals of about ±3–6 points).
  • Trading cost is 2 bps per unit of turnover, a liquid futures-style universe (decision D5).
  • Simulation research only. Nothing here is financial advice.
