Glossary

Researchers

Honest, no edge
Pre-registers one momentum rule (TSMOM-60) and backtests it once, correctly. The market has no edge, so every claim here is pure luck.
Honest, real edge
Same rule, same method, in a market with a genuine hidden trend. These claims are mostly REAL, so a rejection here is a false alarm.
Parameter miner
Tries 120 rule variants and reports the best one. No edge exists.
Parameter miner, real edge
Tries 120 variants in the trending market. Some picks are genuinely good, some just lucky; the oracle decides each one.
Look-ahead bug
Uses today's closing price to decide a trade filled at today's open: information it could not have had.
Full-sample normaliser
Bets on prices returning to the average of the WHOLE sample, future included.
Cost ignorer
A high-turnover reversal rule backtested with zero trading costs. The gross edge is real; after costs it loses money.
Survivorship bias
'Buy the dip' tested only on stocks still listed today. The ones that crashed and were delisted are missing from its data.
Window picker
Tries 11 start dates and reports the period that looks best ("since 2019 it made...").
Asset picker
Runs one rule on 20 assets separately and reports the one where it worked.
